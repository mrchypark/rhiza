# QuePaxa inline-Record microbenchmark

- Base comparison: `benchmarks/results/2026-08-28-archive-refresh/quepaxa-current.txt`
- Change: normal proposal value is carried by the first Record quorum; no separate StageValue quorum.
- Result: in-memory median was about 25.6 ms healthy and 21.9 ms with one peer down versus the prior 25.1 ms and 20.3 ms. The expected QUIC RTT reduction is not visible in this fsync-dominated mock transport benchmark, so Dory latency is the deciding measurement.
